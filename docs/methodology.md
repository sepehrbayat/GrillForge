# GrillForge Methodology

GrillForge is a decision-pressure protocol. It is designed to prevent implementation from starting before the underlying claims have survived meaningful attack.

## Sequence

1. **Claim Stack** — make the hidden logic explicit.
2. **Heat Map** — identify the claims with the largest failure radius.
3. **Five Flames** — attack the critical claims from distinct directions.
4. **Flip Tests** — reverse the framing to expose motivated reasoning.
5. **Proof Skewers** — design the cheapest tests that can change the decision.
6. **Final Plate** — issue a verdict, next move, and stop condition.

## Principle

The protocol spends attention in proportion to the cost of being wrong. A naming preference does not deserve the same scrutiny as an unverified demand assumption or an irreversible architecture choice.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Repeated real-world behavior
2. Payment or meaningful commitment
3. Retention and repeated use
4. Observed workflow friction
5. Stated intent
6. Personal enthusiasm

## Completion

A session is complete when the highest-risk claims are supported, contradicted, or assigned to a bounded experiment—and a clear verdict can be issued.
