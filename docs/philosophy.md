# Philosophy

Most AI coding failures begin before the first line of code.

The agent is usually not missing syntax. The team is missing a shared model of the problem, the user, the constraints, and the evidence.

GrillForge assumes that speed without pressure-testing compounds uncertainty. Its purpose is not to slow builders down. It is to prevent fast movement in the wrong direction.

The central rule is simple:

> Do not reward an idea for sounding coherent. Reward it for surviving attack.

A good outcome is not always approval. Sometimes the highest-value result is a cheap experiment, a smaller scope, a changed approach, or a confident decision to stop.
